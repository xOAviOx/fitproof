SDE-1, Platform (Golang)
Streamforge · Remote (India)

Streamforge runs the video infrastructure behind 120 Indian edtech and media apps. The platform team builds the services that transcode, store, and deliver more than two million videos a day.

You will
1. Build and operate microservices in Golang that talk to each other over gRPC.
2. Own PostgreSQL schemas for the services you write.
3. Deploy and debug workloads on Kubernetes.
4. Improve observability: metrics, traces, and useful alerts.

You have
1. 1–3 years of experience building backend services in production.
2. Strong programming skills in Golang (or strong Java/C++ and a willingness to switch in your first month).
3. Experience with gRPC or REST APIs between services.
4. Working knowledge of Kubernetes and Docker.
5. Good SQL skills, ideally with PostgreSQL.

Bonus points
- Kafka or another streaming system.
- Experience with GCP.

This is a fully remote role, but you must be based in India and legally authorized to work here; we cannot sponsor visas.

CTC: ₹18–26 LPA, plus ESOPs.
