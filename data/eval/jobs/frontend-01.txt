Frontend Engineer (React)
Plotline Learning · Bengaluru · Hybrid

We build live-class software for 1,500 coaching institutes. Our frontend team owns the teacher dashboard and the student app on the web.

What you will work on
- Build product features in React and TypeScript, from design hand-off to release.
- Manage complex client state with Redux.
- Write accessible, responsive layouts in HTML and CSS that work on low-end Android phones.
- Write unit tests with Jest and React Testing Library.
- Profile and fix slow pages.

What you bring
- A strong grasp of JavaScript and TypeScript.
- At least one non-trivial React project you can walk us through.
- Solid HTML and CSS, including flexbox and grid.
- Experience with Redux or a similar state library.
- Familiarity with Jest.

Nice to have: Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and an eye for design.

Eligibility: 2024 or 2025 graduates with a B.E./B.Tech, B.Sc, or BCA degree. Applicants should be willing to work from our Bengaluru office two days a week.

Salary: ₹9–13 LPA.
