Frontend Developer Intern – Remote
Openshelf (open-source library management)

Openshelf is a small, fully remote team building open-source software that public libraries in India use to lend books. This is a 3-month paid internship with a mentor.

You'll help us rebuild the catalogue search in React, fix bugs reported by librarians, and improve accessibility so that screen reader users can find and reserve books on their own.

We'd love to hear from you if:
- You're comfortable with JavaScript and have built something with React.
- You care about accessibility and know the basics of semantic HTML and ARIA.
- You use Git and are happy to work in public on GitHub.

It's a plus if you have contributed to open source before or have used Vue.

Who can apply: students graduating in 2026 or 2027, from any degree programme. The internship is remote, but you must be in India for payroll reasons.

Stipend: ₹25,000/month for 3 months, 20 hours a week.
