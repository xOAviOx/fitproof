Analytics Intern (Remote, 6 months)
Greenleaf Agritech

- Clean and combine spreadsheets from field teams in Excel.
- Write SQL queries against our Postgres reporting database.
- Automate recurring reports using Python or R.

What you need
- Excel: formulas, pivot tables, and charts.
- Basic SQL.
- Either Python or the R language for data analysis.

Good to have
- Any experience with ETL tools or scheduling scripts.

Eligibility: students or recent graduates (2025 or 2026) from any discipline. You need to be available for 6 months starting July 2026.

Stipend: ₹20,000 per month. Remote within India.
